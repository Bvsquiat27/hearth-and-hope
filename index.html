Our promise

Hearth & Hope exists to honor mothers and protect life — without treating women as problems to solve.

Dignity for women

You are brave and valued. We never shame past choices. No “you should have…” — only next steps that respect you.

Support for life

We support life as Christ intended: practical help, community, and hope. Faith appears as encouragement you can skip.

No abortion referrals

We do not list, link to, or refer to Planned Parenthood or any abortion clinic. Period.

Your privacy

This prototype keeps everything on your device. We draft messages you approve; your email or SMS app does the sending. We don’t upload your form to a server.

Important disclaimer

Hearth & Hope provides general information and connection tools only. It is not a substitute for medical care, legal advice, or emergency services. Always talk with a qualified clinician about your health. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911 (or your local emergency number).

Directory entries are fictional placeholders for product testing — a starter network across all 50 states + DC. Enter any town or ZIP to find the nearest listed centers (not every municipality has its own unique entry).
